选型与安装中的常见问题解答

Answers to common selection and installation questions

如何选择热电偶的类型（K/J/S/B型）？How do I choose a thermocouple type (K/J/S/B)?

主要根据测温范围、环境介质及精度要求确定，欢迎联系工程师获取一对一选型建议。

Selection depends on temperature range, process media, and required accuracy — contact our engineers for 1-on-1 guidance.

铠装热电偶和装配式热电偶有什么区别？Armored vs. assembly thermocouples — what's the difference?

铠装热电偶整体密封、耐振动性更强；装配式热电偶安装维护更便捷，适合通用场合。

Armored types are fully sealed and more vibration-resistant; assembly types are easier to install/maintain for general use.

产品的质保期是多久？What's the warranty period?

标准产品质保期为12个月，质保期内非人为损坏免费维修或更换。

Standard products carry a 12-month warranty covering free repair or replacement for non-user-caused defects.
