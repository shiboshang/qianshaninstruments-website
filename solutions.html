应用领域Applications

快速热电偶是熔融金属生产中判定温度的关键工具，以下按用途与行业分类介绍其典型应用

Fast-response thermocouples are the key tool for judging temperature in molten-metal production — typical applications are organized below by use and by industry

用途分类By Application

快速热电偶通过一次性偶头浸入熔体，3~5 秒读取温度，用于熔融金属生产关键节点的温度判定，无需永久安装。

A consumable tip is dipped into the melt to read temperature in 3–5 seconds — used to judge temperature at key points in molten-metal production, with no permanent installation.

出钢 / 出铁温度判定Tapping Temperature Check

转炉、电炉出钢前及高炉出铁时测温，决定出炉时机

Measured before tapping a converter or EAF, and when casting hot metal from a blast furnace, to decide tapping timing

精炼过程控温Refining Control

钢包、LF / RH 精炼环节跟踪钢水温度，指导加热与合金化

Tracks steel temperature during ladle, LF and RH refining to guide heating and alloying

连铸浇注控温Continuous Casting Control

中间包、钢包钢水温度抽测，保障拉速与坯壳质量

Spot-checks tundish and ladle temperature to protect casting speed and shell quality

熔炼配料控温Melting & Charge Control

铸造及有色熔炼炉内熔体测温，控制配料与浇注温度

Measures melt temperature in foundry and non-ferrous furnaces to control charging and pouring

工艺研究与标定Research & Calibration

实验炉测温、科研及现场仪表比对标定

Lab-furnace measurement, research use, and on-site instrument cross-checks

钢铁冶金Iron & Steel

从高炉炼铁到转炉、电炉炼钢，再到炉外精炼与连铸，温度是全流程的核心控制参数，快速热电偶是现场判温的主力工具。

From blast-furnace ironmaking through BOF/EAF steelmaking to secondary refining and continuous casting, temperature is the core control parameter — and fast-response thermocouples are the workhorse for on-floor readings.

铸造Foundry & Casting

铸铁、铸钢车间在熔炼、球化 / 孕育处理及浇注前均需快速判温，以稳定铸件质量、减少废品。

Iron and steel foundries need quick readings during melting, nodularisation/inoculation, and just before pouring to stabilise casting quality and cut scrap.

Temperature control during copper, aluminium, lead and zinc melting and refining directly affects composition, energy use and yield.

铁合金Ferroalloy
