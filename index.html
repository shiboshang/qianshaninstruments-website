关于我们About Us

千山仪表（QianShan Instrument）是一家专注于快速热电偶研发、制造与销售的高新技术企业。公司拥有专业的工程师团队与精密加工产线，主营 KS、KR、KB、KW 四大系列消耗式快速热电偶，广泛应用于炼钢、炼铁、铸造及有色金属熔炼等行业的钢水、铁水快速测温。我们坚持"品质第一"的原则，产品通过 ISO 9001、CE 等认证，测温响应快、批次一致性好，满足冶金现场严苛的测温需求。千山仪表致力于为客户提供从选型咨询、非标定制到售后服务的一站式快速测温解决方案。

QianShan Instrument is a high-tech enterprise dedicated to the R&D, manufacturing, and sales of fast-response thermocouples. Backed by an experienced engineering team and precision production lines, we specialize in the KS, KR, KB and KW series of consumable fast-response thermocouples, widely used for molten steel and iron measurement across steelmaking, ironmaking, foundry and non-ferrous smelting. Guided by a "Quality First" principle, our products are certified to ISO 9001 and CE standards, delivering fast readings and consistent batch quality for demanding metallurgical sites. QianShan Instrument is committed to providing a one-stop fast-measurement solution — from selection consulting and custom engineering to after-sales support.
