关于我们About Us

千山仪表（QianShan Instrument）是一家专注于工业温度测量产品研发、制造与销售的高新技术企业。公司拥有专业的工程师团队与精密加工产线，主营铠装热电偶、装配式热电偶、热电阻、温度变送器及热套管等产品，广泛应用于电力、冶金、石化、建材、玻璃等行业。我们坚持"品质第一"的原则，产品通过 ISO 9001、CE 等认证，并可提供防爆型号，满足不同工况的严苛要求。千山仪表致力于为客户提供从选型咨询、非标定制到售后服务的一站式温度测量解决方案。

QianShan Instrument is a high-tech enterprise dedicated to the R&D, manufacturing, and sales of industrial temperature sensing products. Backed by an experienced engineering team and precision production lines, we specialize in armored thermocouples, assembly thermocouples, RTDs, temperature transmitters, and thermowells, widely used across the power, metallurgy, petrochemical, building materials, and glass industries. Guided by a "Quality First" principle, our products are certified to ISO 9001 and CE standards, with explosion-proof models available for demanding environments. QianShan Instrument is committed to providing a one-stop temperature measurement solution — from selection consulting and custom engineering to after-sales support.
